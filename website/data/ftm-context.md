# Fill The Map Northern Vietnam Context

This website prototype designs Northern Vietnam trips for Fill The Map with Jude.

Brand positioning:
- Founder-led premium adventure travel
- Five-star service attitude in raw, authentic places
- Warm, specific, confident tone
- Strongest differentiation: Jude's hospitality background + local depth

Current tour building blocks and pricing baselines:

## 2-Day Ninh Binh Escape
- Best for: quick getaway, karst landscapes, pagodas, boat rides
- Highlights: Bai Dinh Pagoda, Trang An, Tam Coc, Hang Mua, Hoa Lu
- Estimated per person:
  - Smart Value: USD 60-90
  - Premium Comfort: USD 135-195
  - Luxury Private: USD 245-390

## 3-Day Ha Long Bay Cruise
- Best for: iconic bay scenery, cruise comfort, kayaking, caves
- Highlights: Surprising Cave, Titov Island, Luon Cave, floating village
- Estimated per person:
  - Smart Value: USD 250-325
  - Premium Comfort: USD 325-415
  - Luxury Private: USD 425-640

## 4-Day Sapa Mountain Retreat
- Best for: mountain air, terraces, ethnic villages, moderate trekking
- Highlights: Cat Cat Village, Fansipan, Muong Hoa Valley, Lao Chai, Ta Van
- Estimated per person:
  - Smart Value: USD 220-310
  - Premium Comfort: USD 365-515
  - Luxury Private: USD 595-885

## 5-Day Sapa + Mai Chau Adventure
- Best for: terraced rice fields, valley cycling, homestays, local culture
- Highlights: Fansipan, Lao Chai, Ta Van, Muong Hoa Valley, Lac Village, Pom Coong
- Estimated per person:
  - Smart Value: USD 245-355
  - Premium Comfort: USD 415-605
  - Luxury Private: USD 715-1085

## 7-Day Grand Northern Vietnam Journey
- Best for: complete first-time northern Vietnam immersion
- Highlights: Ha Long Bay cruise, Sapa, Fansipan, ethnic homestays, Mai Chau, Ninh Binh
- Estimated per person:
  - Smart Value: USD 560-805
  - Premium Comfort: USD 925-1295
  - Luxury Private: USD 1585-2405

Planning rules:
- Default geography is Northern Vietnam starting from Hanoi.
- Make decisive recommendations from a small number of inputs.
- Present one best-fit itinerary, not three options.
- Include destinations, day flow, iconic sights, hidden gems, stays, transport, restaurants, included services, and price.
- Use current grounded web research for hotels, restaurants, and practical travel details when Gemini is enabled.
- If exact availability is unknown, clearly mark suggestions as subject to confirmation.
